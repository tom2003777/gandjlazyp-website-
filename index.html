The Pratts

Raising horses in Arvada since 1977

George, Jill, Art and Kristina Pratt have lived on a small acreage in Arvada, Colorado since 1977. Over the years they have bred and raised top AQHA horses, including Hottroddin Novel Te (AQHA ROM in Reining and 1991 NRHA Limited Derby winner in St. Paul, Minnesota) and Sundown Sir Dawn (AQHA Champion in Hunter Under Saddle).

The facility

A quiet barn in the middle of the city

The G and J Lazy P is set back in a peaceful spot surrounded by trees, with easy access from 74th and Indiana.

  • Heated 19-stall barn with 12 × 12 box stalls
  • 10-stall barn added in 2008, with its own wash rack and tack room
  • Individual pens with shelter, one horse per pen, plus a separate tack area and outdoor wash rack
  • Indoor arena, 82 × 120 ft, evenly lit with no shadows, sand and rubber footing worked every day
  • Outdoor arena, well-sanded round pen and hot walker
  • Access to the Indiana Equestrian Center, 50 acres of open space and trails
